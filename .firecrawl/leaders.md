### Hare Krishna!

The Sanga Initiative is so honored by the rejuvenating and spiritually uplifting summer we had with all of you! As we prepare for the future of TSI, we as an organization team, are asking for help from YOU! A lot of inspiring and talented TSI members have reached out to the old TSI organization team asking to serve for the retreat team and/or for the overall TSI organization team. We cannot thank you more for your earnest desire to serve this powerful community and we have some opportunities for service as a TSI leader! We ask all of you interested to try to be reflective and thoughtful when answering the questions on the application form and please be honest with your time commitment and desire to serve, and we will take all of this into consideration during the interview process. As TSI is going through a re-organization effort, finding dedicated youth that are willing to put in meaningful time will be very crucial as we pave the way for the next wave of TSI. Once again, we thank you for your desire to strengthen our community and our retreats and we can’t wait to see what the next generation of TSI accomplishes!

[TSI executive team application](https://www.sangainitiative.org/executive-team-application)

_The executive application will be open until November 24th, 2022. Please get your application in before then!_
