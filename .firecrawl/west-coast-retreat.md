# **TSI WEST COAST RETREAT 2026**

Please note that this retreat is for ages 18-30. Our retreat costs include accommodations at a premier all-inclusive resort in Waikiki beach, prasadam, seminars, and all necessary supplies.

### **LOGISTICS**

- **Nearest Airport: Daniel K. Inouye International Airport**

- **Hotel Rooms will be shared with 3 others of the same gender.**

- **Retreat starts on September 9th at 4:00pm & ends on September 13th at 1:00PM.**


Highlights of the West Coast retreat include:

- Staying at a beach front resort

- Deep Sanga

- Engaging guest speakers

- Workshops (art, talent..)

- Water sports

- Kirtan

- Delicious prasadam.

We can’t wait to see you there!
