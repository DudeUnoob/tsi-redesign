# **Mission Trip 2026**

We are organizing an **ISKCON Youth Mission Trip to Brazil (Nov 14th–22nd, 2026)** for TSI youth ages 18–30. This will be a pure seva-based immersion at Nova Gokula Farm, including eco-building projects, trail and campus improvements, large-scale prasadam preparation, daily sadhana, and participation in a three-day kirtan festival. Please consider donating [here](https://sanga-initiative.squarespace.com/donate)!

Highlights of the Mission Trip include:

- Deep Sanga

- Engaging seva

- Kirtan

- Delicious prasadam


As part of the trip, participants will also visit Christ the Redeemer in Rio de Janeiro — one of the New Seven Wonders of the World — along with light sightseeing before heading to the farm.

Cost is only flight + $125 registration (all lodging, meals, and local transportation are covered). Spots are first come, first serve — the first 20 youth who register and book flights secure their place. No skills required — only the desire to serve.

We can’t wait to see you there!

Location: Nova Gokula farm

**You must have a flight itinerary to reserve your spot!**

- Our team will follow up to confirm your booking is complete within 24 hours of registrations

- REMINDER:

  - Arrive in RIO (GIG) between 8:30 AM - 11:00 AMon Saturday, November 14th.

  - Depart from Sao Paulo (GRU) between 8:30 PM - 11:30 PM on Sunday, November 22nd.
