# **TSI Midwest 2026**

Please note that this retreat is for ages 14-30 and will take place **April 10th-12th, from Friday Evening to Sunday Afternoon**. Our costs of this retreat cover prasadam, seminars, and any supplies needed throughout the retreat. Please consider donating [here](https://sanga-initiative.squarespace.com/donate) to help us do more retreats like this in the future!

Highlights of the TSI Midwest retreat include:

- Deep Sanga

- Engaging guest speakers

- Dynamic workshops

- Kirtan

- Delicious prasadam


We can’t wait to see you there!

**NOTE**: Early Bird Registration ends **March 10th, 2026.** Full **refunds** will only be issued **until March 30th, 2026**.
