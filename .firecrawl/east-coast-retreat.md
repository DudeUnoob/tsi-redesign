# **TSI East COAST RETREAT 2026**

Please note that this retreat is for **ages 16-30** and will take place **June 4th-7th, from Thursday Evening to Sunday Afternoon**. Our costs of this retreat cover accomodation, prasadam, seminars, and any supplies needed throughout the retreat. Please consider donating [here](https://sanga-initiative.squarespace.com/donate)!

Highlights of the TSI East Coast retreat include:

- Deep Sanga

- Engaging guest speakers

- Dynamic workshops

- Kirtan

- Delicious prasadam


We can’t wait to see you there!

Location: ISKCON of DC, 10310 Oaklyn Dr, Potomac, MD 20854

**NOTE**: Full **refunds** will only be issued **until May 1st, 2026**.
