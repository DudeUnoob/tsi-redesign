## Our mission

**Our goal is to provide a safe and nurturing environment where youth have the opportunity to deepen their faith in our spiritual path, strengthen former friendships and create new ones, imbibe values that can be carried through daily life, develop a positive service attitude, and have unforgettable fun throughout the whole journey.**

We are so excited to bring you Camp Ignite. Join us for fun-filled days of water sports, group discussions, team building activities, and much more! From swimming to late night bonfires, Camp Ignite offers numerous ways to have fun while keeping Krishna in the center.

Throughout this trip, the kids will be playing sports, engaging in thought-provoking discussions, taking hikes, participating in epic kirtans, and indulging in some of the best prasadam ever! Camp Ignite gives kids the opportunity to meet kids from different states with similar interests and provides a chance to create friendships and memories that last a lifetime.

Register  for amazing memories, lifelong friendships, and an uplifting spiritual step forward. Feel free to [contact us](mailto:kapi@sangainitiative.org, Sruthi.echarry@sangainitiative.org?) for any questions.
