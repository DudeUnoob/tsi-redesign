## Design Context

### Users
Krishna Conscious youth in North America, specifically young devotees associated with ISKCON. They are looking to connect with peers, find spiritual resources, attend retreats and conferences, and become empowered leaders in their community.

### Brand Personality
Spiritual, Empowering, Youthful, Community-Driven.
Emotions: Devotion, connection, inspiration, warmth, and belonging.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm and inviting. Base the palette on an organic, warm cream/off-white background with strong dark typographic contrast (charcoal or the dark blue from the logo). Headings should be bold, uppercase, and structured (reminiscent of the existing impact/condensed fonts). Let vibrant photography from events and retreats provide the primary color accents. Avoid cold, purely corporate aesthetics—the design should feel rooted in spiritual community and tradition while remaining accessible to youth.

### Design Principles
1. **Community First**: Highlight people, gatherings, and connection through rich imagery and accessible layouts.
2. **Warmth & Grounding**: Use a warm color palette (creams, off-whites) that feels organic and welcoming, paired with strong, legible typography.
3. **Clarity of Purpose**: Ensure calls to action (events, donate, resources) are distinct but harmonious with the spiritual focus.
4. **Youthful Devotion**: Balance traditional spiritual elements with a clean, modern interface that appeals to a younger, next-generation audience without losing its roots.
